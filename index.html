About this app

CodeSnap scans QR codes and barcodes live with the camera or from a gallery image, and makes codes for text, URLs, Wi-Fi networks (including open networks), and contacts. Share the results, save them to your gallery, or find them again in your on-device scan history. No account required.

Privacy Policy for CodeSnap

Effective 26 September 2026

At a glance

Not collected
  • No account or sign-up
  • No personal data collected by the app
  • No sale of data
  • Scan history stays on your device
Processed by third parties
  • Advertising ID and ad interaction data (Google AdMob)
  • Anonymous crash diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

1. What we don't collect

CodeSnap is a scanner and generator app. To be clear about what it does not do:

  • No account or sign-up: there is no registration, login, or user profile.
  • No collection of personal data by the app itself: we don't ask for your name, email, phone number, or contacts, and we don't upload the content you scan or generate.
  • No sale of personal data. We don't sell or rent data about you to anyone.
  • Your scan and generation history is stored only on your device, in local app storage. You can delete it at any time from the app's history screen, or by clearing the app's data in system settings.

Some data is processed by third-party services the app integrates (advertising and crash analytics), as described below.

2. Camera

The camera permission is used only for live scanning: frames are decoded in real time to find and read QR codes and barcodes.

  • Camera frames are processed on your device.
  • Frames are never recorded to video or stored as images.
  • Frames are never transmitted off your device for scanning purposes.

The flashlight toggle only controls the camera flash for scanning in low light.

3. Images (gallery scans and exports)

When you choose to decode a code from an image in your gallery, or to export/save a generated code as an image file, that image is processed locally on your device. Image contents aren't uploaded to our servers. CodeSnap has no backend servers that receive your images.

4. Advertising

CodeSnap shows ads, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the Advertising ID and device/ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising through your device's Google Play settings.

See Google's privacy policy for details: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Buying Premium removes ads, but it doesn't change the data practices described here beyond no longer requesting ads.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

CodeSnap uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash and diagnostic data, for example stack traces, device/OS version, and the app version at the time of a crash. That data is used only to find and fix bugs. It isn't used to identify you personally.

See Google Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Children

CodeSnap isn't directed at children under 13. The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service the app uses, please contact us and we'll take appropriate action.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent by email to developer.hadiawali@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The current version always lives on this page. The effective date above tells you when this version started applying.
