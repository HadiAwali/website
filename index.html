CodeSnap

Kotlin · Android

A privacy-first QR code and barcode scanner and generator for Android — everything runs on your device.

Kotlin · XML · CameraX · Firebase

What it does

CodeSnap scans QR codes and barcodes live with the camera or from a gallery image, and generates codes for text, URLs, Wi-Fi networks (including open networks), and contacts — results can be shared, saved to the gallery, and revisited in an on-device scan history. No account required.

Under the hood

  • Native Kotlin with classic XML/View layouts, CameraX for the camera pipeline, decoding on-device
  • 11 interface languages — English, Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Spanish
  • Ads by Google AdMob with an optional Premium purchase that removes them; Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting
  • Status: coming to Google Play (not yet released)

Privacy Policy

No account or sign-up is required. Your scan and generation history stays on your device. Camera frames are processed locally and never uploaded. Ads by Google AdMob and crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics are the only third-party processing.

NoteBank

Flutter

A multi-platform Markdown note app for Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS, with folders, tags, teams, and export built in.

Flutter · Dart · Firebase · SQLite

What it does

NoteBank lets you write, organize, and export Markdown notes across every major platform, with folders, tags, templates, and team sharing.

Under the hood

  • Built with Flutter/Dart, targeting Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS (web planned later)
  • Firebase for Auth, Firestore, Storage, FCM, and Crashlytics; SQLite for local storage
  • Export to .md, .txt, .html, and .pdf; split-view editor and viewer
  • v1.0 is Firebase-first; a self-hosted backend option is a future goal, not part of this release

Privacy Policy

Last updated: draft. This describes what NoteBank collects and how it's used.

Information collected

  • Account details (email) for sign-in via Firebase Authentication
  • Note content, tags, and folder structure, stored in Firestore and Storage to enable sync across your devices
  • Device push tokens, to deliver notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Crash and performance diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics
  • A local copy of your notes is also kept on-device in SQLite

How it's used

Data is used to sync notes across your devices, support team sharing, and diagnose crashes. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Team sharing

Notes shared with a team are visible to the members you add. Removing a member revokes their access going forward.

Your controls

Exported notes leave the app in a plain file you control. You can delete notes, leave a team, or delete your account from Settings.

Kemakzi

Unity

An arcade-style FPV drone game with real physics-based flight — Acro, Angle, and Horizon modes, keyboard or touch.

Unity · C# · Rigidbody physics

What it does

Kemakzi puts you in control of an FPV-style drone with genuine flight physics, rather than an arcade approximation of one.

Under the hood

  • Physics-based flight using a Rigidbody, with per-motor thrust on a DJI F450 X-frame layout via AddForceAtPosition
  • Three flight modes — Acro, Angle, and Horizon
  • Dual input support: keyboard/mouse and touch joysticks
  • Smoothed camera follow and yaw orbit, plus a debug visualizer for thrust vectors and flight paths

Privacy Policy

Last updated: draft. This describes what Kemakzi collects and how it's used.

Information collected

  • Local settings and progress (control scheme, flight mode) are stored on-device only
