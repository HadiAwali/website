CodeSnap Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the CodeSnap app handles information. Canonical location of this document: https://hadiawali.github.io/website/codesnap/privacy/

1. What we don't collect

CodeSnap is a scanner and generator app. To be clear about what it does not do:

  • No account or sign-up: there is no registration, login, or user profile.
  • No collection of personal data by the app itself: we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or contacts, and we do not upload the content you scan or generate.
  • No sale of personal data. We do not sell or rent data about you to anyone.
  • Scan and generation history is stored only on your device, in local app storage, and can be deleted at any time from the app's history screen (or by clearing the app's data in system settings).

Some data is processed by third-party services that the app integrates — advertising and crash analytics — as described below.

2. Camera

The camera permission is used only for live scanning: frames are decoded in real time to find and read QR codes and barcodes.

  • Camera frames are processed on your device.
  • Frames are never recorded to video or stored as images.
  • Frames are never transmitted off your device for scanning purposes.

The flashlight toggle only controls the camera flash for scanning in low light.

3. Images (gallery scans and exports)

When you choose to decode a code from an image in your gallery, or to export/save a generated code as an image file, that image is processed locally on your device. Image contents are not uploaded to our servers — CodeSnap has no backend servers that receive your images.

4. Advertising

CodeSnap displays ads, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the Advertising ID and device/ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising through your device's Google Play settings.

See Google's privacy policy for details: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchasing Premium removes ads, but does not change the data practices described here beyond no longer requesting ads.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

CodeSnap uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash and diagnostic data — for example, stack traces, device/OS version, and the app version at the time of a crash. This data is used only to find and fix bugs. It is not used to identify you personally.

See Google Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Children

CodeSnap is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent by email to developer.hadiawali@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The current version always lives at the canonical URL above; the effective date below tells you when this version started applying.
